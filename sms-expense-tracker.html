Guide: SMS tracking

Bank alerts become entries, after you agree

When a bank, card or wallet sends you an alert, Money SpendOptic can read it on your phone and add the transaction, so you do not have to type it. It starts only after you agree on an explanation screen, and you can still add spending by hand.

How it works

SMS tracking is a core feature of the app and free. You turn it on from the card on Trans or from More, SMS tracking. The app first explains what it does, and Android asks for permission to read and receive SMS only after you tap Agree. If you say no, nothing is read and you can keep adding entries by hand.

Set it up

  • 1. Agree. Read the screen and tap Agree and turn on.
  • 2. Choose how far back to read. Today only, 1, 3 or 6 months.
  • 3. Choose how entries are added. Add them for me, or Ask me first.
  • 4. Match each bank sender to one of your accounts. Nothing new is created until you confirm.

What it reads and what it keeps

It uses only transaction alerts from banks, cards and wallets it knows, and senders you approve. Messages from people, your contacts and one-time passwords are skipped: they are not kept and not used. A sender the app does not know is shown by name only, and its messages are read only if you say it is a bank or wallet.

The message is read on your phone and its text is thrown away as soon as it has been read. The app keeps only the amount, date, shop or payee, account digits, category and a code that stops the same message being added twice. Nothing is sent to us, and the app never sends SMS. Read the full wording in the privacy policy.

When an alert arrives

If the app is sure of the account and the category and you chose Add them for me, the transaction is added and you get a notification with Undo for 24 hours. Otherwise it waits in To review, where you can Add, Edit or Ignore it. Paying your card bill from your bank is added as one transfer to the card, not as an expense and an income.

Payment app notifications (optional)

Some payments, such as UPI Lite or a wallet, never send a bank SMS. If you want, switch on Payment app notifications in More, SMS tracking, then allow Notification access for the app on Android's own page. The app then reads the payment notifications of the apps you select, on your phone only. The bank and payment apps it recognises (for example Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM, CRED, Amazon Pay and major banks) that you have installed are ticked the first time, and you can tick any other app under Choose apps. Notifications from apps you did not select are not opened, and offers and reminders are skipped. A bank debit and a wallet credit of the same amount within 15 minutes wait together as one Transfer card for you to confirm. If an app is missing, Find a missing app in Choose apps lists, for two minutes and on your phone only, the apps that post a rupee alert.

The notification text is thrown away as soon as it has been read and is never saved or sent to us. If the same payment also came as a bank SMS, the two become one entry. A notification that no SMS matches waits in To review for you to pick the account. After you confirm the same account for an app three payments in a row, that app's later notifications are added on their own, with Undo for 24 hours. It is off until you turn it on, and you can switch it off any time. It works on its own: you do not need SMS tracking to use it, and waiting items show in To review on Trans and under More, SMS tracking. Entries reach another device only when you back up to Google Drive and restore there.

Your control

Open More, SMS tracking to turn it off, or to turn it off and delete its saved records. Your transactions stay. You can also take the SMS permission away in your phone's settings at any time.

Questions

Is my message saved or sent anywhere?

No. The text is thrown away as soon as it has been read. Only the amount, date, shop or payee, account digits and category are kept, and nothing is sent to us.

Does it read messages from people or one-time passwords?

No. Messages from people, your contacts and one-time passwords are skipped, not kept and not used.

What if my bank is not recognised?

In More, SMS tracking, Senders and accounts, add the sender by the name your bank's alerts show. Its messages are read only after you say it is a bank or wallet.

Can I check the original message?

Yes. Open an entry that came from an SMS and tap Show message. The app reads it from your phone at that moment and keeps no copy.

Do I have to use it?

No. It starts only after you agree, and you can add every entry by hand.
