Privacy policy

Money SpendOptic is a money-manager app for Android. This page explains what the app does with your data. The short version: your money data stays on your phone, and we do not run a server that collects it.

Data stored on your phone

Your accounts, transactions, categories, notes, photos you attach and settings are saved only on your device. We never receive them. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage, deletes them.

Backups

You can save a backup file on your phone. If you choose Google Drive, the backup is sent to your own Google Drive account using your Google sign-in. It is stored in your Drive, not on our servers, and we cannot read it.

The app asks Google for one permission only: the drive.file scope. It lets the app see only the files it created itself (the backup files, which also hold the documents you keep on accounts' Docs tabs, and, if you turn on Include photos for Drive, your photos and attachments in a Photos folder), and nothing else in your Drive. You can remove this access at any time in your Google Account (Security › Third-party access) or by signing out in the app.

When you connect, the app also reads the e-mail address of the Google account you chose, from Google Drive, and keeps it on your phone only, so it can show which account is used for backups. It is not sent to us or to anyone else, and it is removed when you disconnect.

SMS data and financial information

Automated SMS tracking is a core feature of Money SpendOptic, activated only after your explicit consent in More › SMS tracking or on the Transactions screen. The app first shows a screen that explains what it does, and asks Android for permission to read and receive SMS only after you tap Agree. You can say no and keep adding transactions by hand.

What it reads. Only transaction alerts from banks, cards and wallets that the app knows (for example Kotak, SBI, HDFC, Axis, Federal Bank, IDFC, ICICI, Google Pay, PhonePe and CRED) and senders you approve. A bank outside India (such as Monzo or Chase) is shown by name and its messages are read only if you tap Yes, and a numeric short code is never read. Messages from people, your contacts and one-time passwords are skipped: they are not kept and not used. A sender the app does not know is shown by name only, and its messages are read only if you say it is a bank or wallet.

What it keeps. A message is read on your phone and its text is thrown away as soon as it has been read. The app keeps only the amount, date, shop or payee, account digits, category and a code used to avoid adding the same message twice. New alerts are read as they arrive; older messages in your inbox are read once, only for the months you choose when you set it up. If you choose Show message on a transaction, the app reads that message from your phone's inbox at that moment and does not copy it.

Where it goes. Nowhere. The SMS data is used only to add and sort your transactions. It is not shared, not sold and not used for ads, and it is not sent to us. The saved details are part of your backups, so if you choose Google Drive backup they go to your own Drive, in the same way as the rest of your data (see Backups above).

Your control. Turn SMS tracking off, or turn it off and delete its saved records, in More › SMS tracking. You can also take the SMS permission away in your phone's settings at any time.

Payment app notifications (optional)

Payment app notifications is optional and off until you turn it on in More › Payment app notifications and then switch on Notification access for this app on Android's own page. You can say no and keep using SMS tracking or adding transactions by hand.

What it reads. Only the notifications of the apps you select. The first time, the bank and payment apps the app recognises (for example Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM, CRED, Amazon Pay, PayPal, Venmo, Wise, Revolut and major banks) that are installed are ticked, and you can tick or untick any app in More › Payment app notifications › Choose apps. To show that list, the app looks at which apps on your phone have a launcher icon; this list stays on your phone and is not sent anywhere. Notifications from apps you did not select are not opened, and even from a selected app only a message that says a payment was made or received is used. The one exception is Find a missing app: only for two minutes after you tap it in Choose apps, the app checks in memory whether another app's notification mentions a money amount, keeps only that app's name until you close the list, and drops the text at once. If you tick an e-mail app such as Gmail, its notifications are checked in memory on your phone and dropped at once unless they look like a bank or payment receipt (an amount plus account digits or a transaction word); nothing is saved from the others. Offers, reminders and requests are skipped.

What it keeps. The notification text is read on your phone only and is thrown away as soon as it has been read: it is never saved and never sent to us or to anyone else. The app keeps only the amount, date, payee, the name of the app, account digits, a reference and a code used to avoid adding the same payment twice. A notification that matches a bank SMS adds its details to that entry once; any other notification waits in To review for you to confirm. After you confirm the same account for an app three payments in a row, that app's later notifications are added on their own, and you can undo them.

Where it goes. Nowhere. It is not shared, not sold and not used for ads. The saved details are part of your backups, so entries reach another device only when you back up to Google Drive and restore there (see Backups above).

Your control. Turn it off in More › Payment app notifications, or switch off Notification access for the app in your phone's settings at any time.

Shared text (optional)

Shared text is optional and only happens when you choose it: in another app (SMS, WhatsApp, Gmail) you select text, tap Share and pick Money SpendOptic. The app needs no permission for this and reads only the text you shared, never other messages. For apps without a Share button, such as WhatsApp, you can copy the message and tap paste text on the Add page: the app then reads the clipboard only at that tap, and only to show you the Shared text page.

What it keeps. The text is read on your phone and shown to you on the Shared text page, so you can check each transaction before anything is added. It is kept in memory only while that page is open, is never saved to the app's storage or to backups, and is never sent to us or to anyone else. Only the transactions you tick and add are saved, like any transaction you type yourself.

Avoiding double entries. To avoid adding a payment twice, the app compares a bank reference number in the text with the details it already keeps from SMS tracking and payment app notifications, on your phone. For each payment you add from shared text, the app also keeps its bank reference number, amount and direction (not the text), only on your phone and in your backups, so the same message is noticed if it is shared again. It is removed when you delete that transaction.

Where it goes. Nowhere. Shared text is not used for ads and is not shared or sold.

Computer page (optional)

If you turn on the computer page, your phone shows its data to a browser on the same Wi-Fi network. The data goes directly between your phone and your computer. It does not pass through the internet or our servers.

You can sign in to the computer page by scanning a QR code shown on it. The app uses Google's code scanner, part of Google Play services, to read the code. The app needs no camera permission and does not store or send the picture. The QR code works once and stops working after two minutes.

Other permissions

  • Notifications: for reminders you set, and for tips and news from us if you allow them. The app asks once, after Welcome; you can say no and change it any time.
  • Read and receive SMS: only if you turn on SMS tracking, to turn bank, card and wallet alerts into transactions on your phone (see SMS data and financial information above). The app never sends SMS.
  • Notification access: only if you turn on Payment app notifications, to read payment notifications of the apps you select on your phone (see Payment app notifications above). The app never posts to or changes other apps' notifications.
  • Internet and network state: for the Google Drive backup, ads, analytics, notifications, Premium and app updates, and for the optional computer page on your Wi-Fi.
  • Start at boot, background service, keep awake: to bring back your reminders after a restart, and to keep the computer page running while it is on.
  • Biometric / screen lock: to lock the app, if you turn that on. The app never sees your fingerprint or face data.

Ads

The app shows ads using Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address and similar device information to show and measure ads. See how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Premium and payments (Google Play Billing)

Premium is bought through Google Play Billing. Google handles the payment: we never see or store your card or payment details. Google tells the app whether your purchase or subscription is active, and the app keeps that status on your phone. Your purchases are also covered by Google's Privacy Policy and the Google Play terms.

Google Play services

The app saves one number, the day you first installed it, in Google Play services' Block Store on your phone. Android keeps it if you uninstall and install the app again, so the 30 free days are not restarted. It is not sent to us and is not backed up to the cloud.

The app uses Google Play to ask for a store review and to check for and install app updates. Google may process device and usage information to do this, under its own privacy policy.

Analytics and messages (Firebase)

The app uses Google Firebase Analytics to count how the app is used, for example which features are opened. It does not send your transactions, amounts, account names or notes. Firebase may collect an app instance ID and device information such as model, Android version, country and language. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, analytics starts only after you agree in the consent form.

The app also sets a few labels on the analytics profile, with fixed values only: whether you have a paid Premium plan (yes or no), the Style you chose and the language you chose. It also records how much revenue an ad impression earned (the ad's own revenue figure, not your money) and a few usage steps, such as the Welcome page you reached or that a file was shared. None of this contains your transactions, amounts, names or notes.

The app can also receive tips and news from us through Firebase Cloud Messaging. You can turn this off in Settings › Tips and news. See Firebase privacy information.

Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before showing personalised ads. You can change your choice later in Settings › Privacy choices.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell your data. Apart from AdMob ads, Firebase, Google Play (Billing, reviews and updates) and the Google Drive backup you choose (all described above), the app shares nothing with third parties.

Your choices and deletion

Because your data is only on your phone (and your own Drive backup, if you made one), you control it: delete transactions in the app, clear the app's storage, uninstall it, or delete the backup file from your Drive. You can also turn off SMS tracking, delete its saved records, or remove the SMS permission in your phone's settings.

This website and fonts

This website uses Google Analytics to count visits: which pages are opened, roughly where visitors come from (country, the site that linked here) and the kind of device and browser. It runs only if you tap Accept on the cookie banner; if you decline or do nothing, no analytics script loads and no cookies are set. Google Analytics then sets its own cookies (named _ga) and Google processes the data under its privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy). We keep the data for 2 months, do not use Google signals or ads features, and never link it to the app. You can change your choice at any time with Cookie settings at the bottom of each page. Your choice itself is kept only in your browser. The site's other script runs the carousel and the computer-view demo in your browser and sends nothing anywhere. No third-party fonts are loaded.
