Guide: Cards, loans and EMIs

Card bills and EMIs, in one place

See what you owe on each credit card, which statement is open or due, and what is left on every loan, with reminders before the date.

Credit cards

Add an account in a Card group, then fill in the settlement date (the day each statement starts), the payment date (the day the bill is due) and, if you like, the account you pay from and the card limit. Spending on the card raises what you owe, and the card page shows its latest statements as chips.

Each statement is Open (not closed yet), Due (closed, something still owed) or Paid. The Accounts tab shows the Due or Paid chip.

Loans and EMIs

A loan is an account in a Loan group. Fill in the EMI amount, the number of EMIs and the first EMI date. Its page then shows what is left to pay, the EMIs paid, the next one and when the loan ends. Pay each EMI as a Transfer from your bank account to the loan.

For an old loan, type what you still owe today as the balance and set the first EMI date. EMIs due before today show as paid by the date.

Installments

An installment splits one amount into equal monthly parts. Choose Installment in Add and the number of months. Parts can be added all now, or each on its own date, and show as (2/4) after the note. Loan EMIs also appear in the Installments tab.

Reminders

The Reminders bell covers card bills, EMIs and low balance, only for the accounts you pick. It is also on the computer view.

Questions

Does the app pay my card bill?

No. It records what you owe and when. Pay in your bank or card app.

Can I track more than one card?

Yes. Add each card as its own account in a Card group.

What about a loan that already has payments?

Create it with the amount you still owe as a negative balance and set the first EMI date. EMIs due before today show as paid by the date.

Is my data uploaded?

No. Your records stay on your phone.
