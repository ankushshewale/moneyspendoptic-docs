Features

Everything the app does.

Tap any feature to see its screen below.

  • Daily transactionsEvery entry by day, with the month’s income, expense and total on top.
  • InsightsSpending by category, a six-month trend and compare with last month.
  • Notes that fill themselvesStart typing a note and pick a past entry - category and account fill in for you.
  • Add severalOne account, many rows: enter a whole bill or a trip in one go.
  • TagsTag entries across categories - a trip, a person, a project - and find them again.
  • Repeat and installmentsRent, EMIs and subscriptions add themselves, and you see what’s coming up next.
  • BudgetsA limit per category with clear over and under bars.
  • Accounts and cardsGroups, balances that add up, card statements and transfers.
  • Search and filtersSearch your notes; filter by type, account, category, amount and period.
  • Backup and privacyBackup file, your own Google Drive, Excel import - your data stays yours.
