Money SpendOptic

A private money manager for Android. Keep track of what you earn and spend, plan with budgets, and keep your data on your own phone.

What the app does

  • Record income, expenses and transfers between your accounts.
  • Group money into accounts, categories and tags, and see totals by day, month and year.
  • Set budgets, repeats and installments, and get reminders.
  • Search your entries, add photos, and view charts and reports.
  • Available in English, Hindi and Marathi, with light, dark and colour styles.

Your data stays with you

Your money data is saved on your phone. We run no server that collects it.

Google Drive backup (optional)

You can choose to save a backup of your data to your own Google Drive. The app asks for one Google permission only, drive.file, so it can see and manage only the backup files it created. It cannot see anything else in your Drive. You can remove this access at any time in your Google Account.
