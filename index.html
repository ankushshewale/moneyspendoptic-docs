Features

Everything the app does.

Tap any feature to see its screen below.

  • Daily transactionsEvery entry by day, with the month’s income, expense and total on top.
  • InsightsSpending by category or month bars, a six-month trend and compare with last month.
  • Notes that fill themselvesStart typing a note and pick a past entry - category and account fill in for you.
  • Add severalOne account, many rows: enter a whole bill or a trip in one go. They show as one group you can name and rename.
  • TagsTag entries across categories - a trip, a person, a project - and find them again.
  • RepeatRent, subscriptions and salary add themselves on schedule, and you see what’s coming up next.
  • InstallmentsSplit a big buy into parts: see how many are paid and when the next one is due.
  • BudgetsA limit per category with clear over and under bars.
  • Accounts and cardsGroups, balances that add up, a summary card on every account, card statements, loans, investments and transfers.
  • Card statementsEach card shows what you owe against its limit, every statement billed and unpaid, and a Mark paid button for when you have paid it. Add the card anniversary for an annual fee reminder and a fee-waiver spend tracker.
  • Accounts and groupsCash, bank, cards and more in groups: add, hide, reorder and edit.
  • CategoriesCategories with subcategories for expense and income, each with its own icon to pick, and your own to add.
  • NotesMemos for any day, like a shopping list or a trip plan, as coloured notes you can pin.
  • Search and filtersSearch notes and descriptions, with results under day headings and their notes; filter by type, account, category, amount and period.
  • Backup and privacyBackup file, your own Google Drive, Excel import - your data stays yours.
  • RemindersA bell on the Trans tab lists what needs a look: card bills, overdue bills, loan EMIs, budgets and accounts that may run short for the payments coming. Switch on only the reminders you want, account by account.
  • Assets and liabilitiesTap Assets, Liabilities or Total on the Accounts tab to see every month as a bar from its opening to its closing balance, whether it went up or down, and what moved it by account or by type. The last six months are free.
