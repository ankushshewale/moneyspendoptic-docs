Guide: Computer view

Your phone, on the big screen

Add many entries at once and read Insights, accounts and reminders in your computer's browser. It runs from your phone over your own Wi-Fi, so your records are not uploaded anywhere.

Connect in three steps

  • 1. Start it on the phone. Open More, then Use on a computer, and turn sharing on.
  • 2. Open the address. Type the address the phone shows into any browser on the same Wi-Fi.
  • 3. Sign in. Scan the QR code on the page with the phone and tap Approve, or type the 6-digit code.

The page stays locked until the code is entered. Turning sharing off, or closing the app, stops the address working.

What you can do

Add, add several and edit transactions, look at Insights and Accounts, use the Reminders bell, search notes across months, and in Manage change categories, tags, budgets and repeats, download a backup, and export or import Excel.

Private by design

Your data stays on your phone and your home network. Nothing is uploaded. Use it on a home or office Wi-Fi only: the page is not encrypted, and the app warns you when the network looks public. Both devices must be on the same network.

Premium

The computer view is a Premium tool. It is free for your first 30 days after you install the app. See the app for current prices.

Questions

Do I need an account or the cloud?

No account and no cloud. The computer talks to your phone directly over your Wi-Fi.

How do I disconnect a computer?

Tap Disconnect on the phone, turn sharing off, or tap New code, which signs every connected computer out.

Is it safe on public Wi-Fi?

No. Use it only on your home or office Wi-Fi.

Can I edit from the computer?

Yes: add, add several, edit, duplicate, bookmark and delete transactions. Some settings are managed in the app.
